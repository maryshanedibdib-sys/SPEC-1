Lala Sadi

Leah Sadi (real name), known as "Lala Sadi" or "Lala" is a 23-year-old TikTok star, content creator, singer, and upcoming television executive producer.

Lala Sadi is born on July 26, 2003 at Lebanon, but lives and was raised in Arizon, USA. She studied biochemistry/ pre-med at Arizon State University.
